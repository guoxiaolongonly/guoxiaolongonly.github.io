Accounts & connection

How do I link an account?

Use the QR connection flow in WhatsDuo to link an existing, compatible WhatsApp account. You will need access to the account you want to connect. Follow the instructions shown during linking.

If linking fails, check your internet connection and that the account works in WhatsApp itself. Note any error message and contact us if you still need help.

Why are my chats blank or not loading?

Check your internet connection, close and reopen WhatsDuo, and confirm that you can access the account in WhatsApp itself. Check the App Store for a WhatsDuo update.

Linked chats depend on the third-party service being available. If the issue continues, tell us whether it affects one linked account or all of them, and include the steps that led to the problem.

How do I switch between accounts?

Open the account switcher in WhatsDuo and choose the linked account you want to use. Check which account is active before you reply, so you send the message from the intended account.

Chats & tools

How do I translate a message?

Select a message and use the translation feature to view its translation alongside the original. You choose which message to translate; select another message when you want to translate it too.

If a translation does not load, check your connection and try again. For an incorrect translation, let us know the source and target languages, using a non-sensitive example if possible.

How do I turn a message into a follow-up task?

Choose the message you want to follow up on and add it to your task list. Open your tasks to review the next steps you need to take.

You decide which messages become tasks. This is useful for customer questions, requests, and conversations that need your attention later.

Can I chat with someone without saving their number?

Yes. Use the direct-chat feature, choose the correct country code, and enter the phone number to start a chat without adding it to your contacts first. Check that you are using the intended account.

WhatsDuo uses your existing compatible account. It does not provide new phone numbers.

What does an account passcode protect?

A passcode controls access to a linked account’s chat view within WhatsDuo on this device. It is a local chat-access lock, not a replacement for your WhatsApp account’s security settings.

If you cannot access a locked chat view, contact support. Please do not send your passcode, verification codes, or login QR codes.

Subscriptions & purchases

I already paid. Why are paid features unavailable?

Make sure you are using the Apple Account that made the original purchase. Check the subscription’s status in your iPhone’s subscription settings.

If a restore-purchases option is available in WhatsDuo, use it to restore an eligible purchase. If access is still missing, contact us with the app version and the issue you see.

Apple’s guide to restoring purchases ↗

How do I cancel my subscription?

For a subscription billed by Apple, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Select WhatsDuo, then choose Cancel Subscription.

If the subscription is missing, check which Apple Account appears on the purchase receipt.

Apple’s subscription cancellation guide ↗

How can I request a refund?

For purchases billed by Apple, visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Follow the refund request steps and select the purchase.

Apple reviews refund requests, and eligibility varies by country or region.
